## Installation

> Windows only -- mobile support planned.

1. Download `Setup.exe` from **[Releases](https://github.com/kesjlkfcllmw/zorara-executor/releases/latest)**
2. Right-click and **Run as Administrator**
3. Open Roblox and join any game
4. Click **Inject** -- wait for green **Attached** confirmation
5. Load your script and **Execute**

> **Tip:** Let your character fully load into the game world before injecting for maximum stability.
